For this assignment, I learned how to use SQL to retrieve and organize information from a music database. I used SELECT to display song names, ORDER BY to sort songs, and WHERE to filter results based on specific conditions. I also practiced using AVG to calculate average energy and LIMIT to control the number of results.

The most interesting part was working with two related tables. For questions 6 and 7, I used JOIN to connect songs with artists through the artist_id and id columns. This allowed me to find songs by Post Malone and calculate the average energy of Drake's songs.

For question 8, I used LIKE '%feat.%' to find songs containing "feat." in their titles. This question did not require a JOIN because the information was already stored in the songs table. Another approach could use a JOIN to include artist information, but it would not be necessary to find the song titles.

Overall, this assignment helped me understand how SQL can search, filter, and combine information efficiently without having to manually examine every record.
